## 出版版次與單位的判讀

- 多個矩直徑與σ共用矩輸入和代數關係，不是多份獨立物理證據。
- 不同試次／樣本不能只因標為unaged/aged就當成一個事件的配對。
- 來源推算的加熱溫度不當成量測的初始氣相／液相溫度。
- 紙上印列位數只是數值比對精度，不是量測不確定度。

- This verifies publication-to-current canonical workbook-summary identity, not origin or correctness of every workbook formula/calibration.
- No raw-sensor or TEM-file identity is upgraded by this summary binding.
- Table1 consists of selected source cases; it is not evidence that every SAME/SAME-R trial appears in the paper.
- Lampwick#54unaged and#55aged are different test labels/samples, not two fractions of one trial.
- The paper source and current workbook values remain separate; do not repair the Pyrell#64sigma difference.
- Temperature column BZ is source-calculated T (C) - Eq.2 of lrt; it is not a newly measured bulk cabin gas temperature.
- Moment diameters and sigma_g share underlying moment inputs and algebraic relations. Their matching is lineage evidence, not multiple independent physical confirmations or model validation.
