## 適用限制與消防延伸

- All .dat outputs are numerical, including EarthGravity.
- DBP is a surrogate and does not identify Kapton/Teflon smoke chemistry.
- Saved PSD ordinates have no column/unit header; absolute normalization was not assumed.
- The archived cross-section PSD omits local T,velocity,vapor,all-node spatial fields and largest-node treatment needed for a coupled dynamic run.
