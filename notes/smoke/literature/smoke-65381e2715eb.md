## 適用限制與消防延伸

- The report includes reused excerpts; publication count is not independent experimental replication.
- Appendix F equation 7 has ambiguous sigma_g typography. The executed mass-moment audit does not use it.
- Estimated wire temperature is not a directly measured uniform sample temperature.
- Source workbook densities are fixed author assumptions rather than independently measured aerosol densities.
- Algebraic closure of the twenty stored Dm values is not external validation or an ignition/extinction prediction.
